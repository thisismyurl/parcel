Our story

Started in a garage, still stubborn about it

We started roasting a pound at a time on a converted popcorn machine because the coffee we could buy nearby wasn’t worth the walk. Nine years later the machine is bigger, but the batches are still small on purpose.

Every bean is cupped before it’s bought, every loaf is shaped by hand, and every regular gets remembered. That’s the whole plan.

How we work

Small batches, on purpose

Cupped before we buy

Every lot is tasted blind against three others before it ever makes the truck.

Shaped by hand

No dough sheeter in the building. Every loaf gets the same two hands every morning.

We remember your order

Small enough that the regulars are, well, actually regular to us too.

60-mile sourcing
